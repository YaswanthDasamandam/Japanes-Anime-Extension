# Privacy Policy for Anime Rōmaji & Dual Subtitles

Last updated: September 2026

## 1. Overview
**Anime Rōmaji & Dual Subtitles** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how the Extension handles user data.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected**: The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information (such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, browsing history, or payment info).
- **Local On-Device Processing**: All Japanese text segmentation, Romanization, dictionary lookups, and subtitle displays are performed entirely locally on your device within your browser session.
- **Video & Caption Content**: The Extension reads subtitle/closed-caption tracks exclusively to provide dual subtitles, Rōmaji, and dictionary popovers on the page you are viewing. No video, audio, or caption data is transmitted to any external server or third party.

## 3. Storage and Permissions
- `storage`: Used strictly to save user preferences locally (such as subtitle font scale, preferred display mode, and English toggle state).
- `activeTab` / Host Permissions: Used strictly to detect video player elements and render the subtitle overlay on video streaming websites.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, telemetry, or advertising networks.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
