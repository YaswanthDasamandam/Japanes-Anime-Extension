🎌 Chrome Extension Video Player Test

This page runs a real HTML5 <video> player with native Japanese subtitles (.vtt) for testing the extension.

🧪 How to verify the Extension here:

  1. Load the unpacked extension in Chrome via chrome://extensions (Folder: c:\Users\dysai\MyProjects\Japanes Anime Extension\extension).
  2. Refresh this page. You will see the extension automatically hook into the <video> player.
  3. Play the video: Watch the floating subtitle box light up both Japanese and Rōmaji words in real-time!
  4. Press M to cycle between Dual Mode, Hover-Only Mode, and Furigana Mode.
  5. Press E to toggle the English translation line.
  6. Hover over any word to view its English Pronunciation and definition popup.
  7. Click and drag the subtitle box to reposition it anywhere on your screen.
