🎌 Chrome Extension Video Player Test

This page runs a real HTML5 <video> player with native Japanese subtitles (.vtt) for testing the extension.

Both tracks active

🧪 How to verify the Dual Subtitles & 3-Tier Furigana here:

  1. Load or reload the unpacked extension in Chrome via chrome://extensions (Folder: c:\Users\dysai\MyProjects\Japanes Anime Extension\extension).
  2. Refresh this page. You will see the extension automatically hook into the <video> player and detect both Japanese and English subtitle tracks.
  3. Play the video: Both Japanese and English subtitles appear together in real time!
  4. Press M to switch to Furigana Mode: Notice the 3 tiers:
    • Top: English Pronunciation (Rōmaji)
    • Middle: Japanese Kanji / Kana characters
    • Bottom of each word: English meaning gloss
    • Bottom line: Full English sentence translation
  5. Press E to toggle English subtitles on/off.
  6. Press [ and ] to scale subtitle font size smaller or larger.
  7. Hover over any word to view its interactive Dictionary Popover (English pronunciation badge, JLPT rating, and full definitions).
  8. Click and drag the subtitle box or control pill to reposition anywhere on screen.
